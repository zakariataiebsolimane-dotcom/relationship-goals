Frequently asked questions

What should I get my boyfriend for Christmas if he says he wants nothing?

Get something he uses every day but would never buy himself: a new wallet, a good hoodie or a better cologne. Add a personal touch like an engraved wallet card and it becomes unforgettable.

What is a good sentimental Christmas gift for a boyfriend?

An engraved wallet card with your own message. It is small, he carries it every day, and it says exactly what you want it to say.

What is a good Christmas gift for a new boyfriend?

Keep it thoughtful but not too intense: a cozy hoodie, a card game you can play together, or a fun gift for his hobby.

What are good stocking stuffers for a boyfriend?

Small things he will use every day: an engraved wallet card, an AirTag for his keys, or a What I Love About You book you fill in yourself.

When should I order Christmas gifts on Amazon?

By mid-December for most items. Engraved and personalized gifts take longer, so order those first.
