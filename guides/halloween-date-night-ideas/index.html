Frequently asked questions

What can couples do on Halloween at home?

Carve pumpkins, play a haunted escape room game, have a horror movie marathon with fun popcorn, or make a spooky dinner with themed drinks.

What is a cheap Halloween date idea?

A horror movie night with homemade popcorn and a blanket, or pumpkin carving. Both cost very little and feel special.

What are fun Halloween activities for couples who don't like horror?

A Halloween puzzle, pumpkin carving, baking spooky treats or a cozy movie like a family Halloween classic. Spooky does not have to mean scary.
