Frequently asked questions

What is the most sentimental gift for a boyfriend?

Your own words. A journal you fill in, like Why I Love You, or a jar of love notes. He can keep it forever, and it means more than anything you can buy.

What is a sentimental gift for a husband?

A woven photo blanket, a Spotify plaque with your wedding song, or a Story of Us journal you fill in together.

Do guys like sentimental gifts?

Yes, even if they do not say it. Most men keep notes and small keepsakes for years. The trick is to make it personal, not over the top.

How early should I order personalized gifts?

2 to 3 weeks before the date. Engraved and custom photo gifts are made to order, and they take longer in December.
