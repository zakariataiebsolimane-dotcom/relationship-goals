Frequently asked questions

What is a boo basket?

A Halloween gift basket full of spooky treats and small gifts. The tradition is to leave it at someone's door, ring the bell and run.

What do you put in a boo basket for your boyfriend?

Funny Halloween socks, his favorite candy, a cozy blanket, a horror movie game, a candle and a small love note. 5 or 6 items is enough.

How much should a boo basket cost?

As much as you want. A few small items and candy already make a great basket. It is the idea that counts.

When should I give a boo basket?

Any day in the week before Halloween. Earlier is better, so he can use the blanket and play the game on Halloween night.
