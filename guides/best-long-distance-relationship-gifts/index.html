Frequently asked questions

What is the best gift for a long distance relationship?

Something they use every day. Touch bracelets and touch lamps win because they create a small moment of connection daily, not just on the day you give them.

Do touch bracelets work in different countries?

Yes, as long as both phones have internet. They connect through an app, so 50 km or 5,000 km makes no difference. Check that both phones are supported before buying.

What is a cheap but meaningful long distance gift?

Love notes. A postbox of handwritten notes costs little and often makes people cry, because the value is in your words.

When should I order a long distance Christmas gift?

At least 2 to 3 weeks before Christmas if it ships internationally. December shipping gets slow, and nobody wants a gift that arrives on December 28.
